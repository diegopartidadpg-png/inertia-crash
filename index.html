The Newton’s 1st Law Crash Game® is a game about driving through obstacles and getting power ups to speed your car up. The point of the game is to drive down a ramp and crash into a wall to make your ragdoll player go as far as it can to move on to the next level. You will have a shop to upgrade your car and your player. This game relates to Newton’s 1st law of motion because the car is an object that stays in motion but when it hits the wall or when it hits an object, whatever it hits is the unbalanced opposing force which either stops or slows the car down.
